# insrt — website design plan

## Goal
Introduce insrt as an embedded insurance partner. Explain protection within an existing customer journey and invite partnership conversations.

## Reference research
- Qover: clear API-first platform positioning and lifecycle capabilities.
- Wrisk: industry-specific solutions and customer-centric experiences.
- Eir: transparent, step-by-step partnership process.
- Allina: white-label insurance that preserves the partner's brand and customer relationship.

These references inform information architecture, not copied branding, text or assets.

## Visual direction
Editorial, Scandinavian-inspired identity: warm ivory (#f7f7f0), ink (#242821), electric lime (#d5f46b), oversized typography, generous whitespace and lifestyle photography. Custom lowercase wordmark, rounded controls and restrained motion.

## Homepage
1. Compact navigation and partnership CTA.
2. Hero: “Life happens. Be right there.” Forest photography and illustrative protection card.
3. Partner benefits: relevance, brand ownership, growth.
4. Industry tabs: mobility, retail, fintech, travel.
5. Dark platform section showing the insurance lifecycle and an illustrative integration.
6. Process: discover, design, integrate, evolve.
7. Accessible FAQ accordion.
8. Partnership contact section and footer.

## Functional scope
Mobile navigation, working anchors, keyboard-accessible industry tabs, FAQ accordions, validated enquiry dialog and downloadable enquiry text. No server submission, analytics or nonessential cookies. Prototype limitations explicitly disclosed. No invented clients, regulatory credentials or metrics.

## Technology and validation
React, TypeScript, Vite, Lucide icons and plain CSS. Production build, desktop/mobile browser checks, interactions and accessibility basics. Respect reduced-motion settings.

## Before launch
Confirm products, markets, regulatory status, legal entity, privacy policy, contact destination, approved copy and image rights. Connect a production enquiry endpoint. Product UI and integration examples are illustrative, not documentation.
